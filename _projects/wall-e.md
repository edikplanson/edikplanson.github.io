# WALL-E Animatronic

A full-scale functional replica of WALL-E, entirely designed and built from scratch.

## Overview

I started this project in 2023 after finding an old WALL-E toy.

The goal was to transform the character into a full-size interactive robot.

## Hardware

- ESP32
- PCA9685
- 15 servo motors
- Tracked drive system
- PS5 controller
- DFPlayer Mini
- 3D printed parts

## What I worked on

- Mechanical design
- 3D modeling
- Electronics
- PCB / wiring
- Embedded programming
- Servo control
- Sound integration
- Weathering and finishing
